Followback

See who doesn't follow you back on Instagram, from your own data export.

🔒 Runs entirely in this browser tab. No login, nothing is uploaded, and the page is locked so it cannot make network requests. You can save this page and use it offline.
First: get your data from Instagram (takes a few minutes)
  1. In Instagram, open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information.
  2. Create an export to your device. Under Customize information choose only Followers and following.
  3. Set Format: JSON (not HTML) and any date range — All time is best.
  4. Instagram emails you when it's ready. Download the .zip and drop it below. There's no need to unzip it.

Sort:

    Tap a username to open the profile and unfollow in Instagram, then tick the box. find searches for an account that was renamed. copy copies the username. Mark dead for profiles that say “Sorry, this page isn't available” (usually deleted or banned before the export was made), and keep for accounts you follow on purpose (brands, artists, news). Dead and kept accounts are hidden here and in your next export. Marks are saved in this browser only.

    Followback is open source and also available as a command-line tool. Automated unfollowing is deliberately not included: it breaks Instagram's Terms of Service and can get your account restricted.
